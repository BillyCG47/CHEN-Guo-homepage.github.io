陈果课题组致力于研究世界未解之谜目前专注于研究金字塔的建造。金字塔的建造之谜核心在于，古埃及人究竟如何在缺乏现代机械的情况下，以惊人的精度运输和垒砌重达数吨的巨石，其内部隐藏的密室和复杂结构也仍在不断挑战着考古学家的认知。小组运用了世界领先的克隆技术复活了牛顿、爱因斯坦等人作为小组成员，因此此小组目前可以认为是全球领先的课题组。

The Billy Research Group Dedicated to investigating the world's unsolved mysteries currently focusing on the construction of the pyramids. The core enigma of pyramid building lies in how the ancient Egyptians, without modern machinery , managed to transport and align massive stones weighing several tonnes with astonishing precision . The hidden chambers and intricate structures within continue to challenge archaeologists' understanding. The team has utilised world-leading cloning technology to resurrect figures such as Newton and Einstein as members of the group; as such, this team can currently be regarded as a world-leading research group.
